My story

I'm a mechanical engineering student at NC State with a 4.0 GPA, expected to graduate in 2029. I like projects where I get to design something in CAD and then build it with my own hands.

I'm a member of NC State SolarPack, where I work on the Structures and Business teams of a student-built, street-legal solar car that competes in the Formula Sun Grand Prix. I've helped design the car's high-voltage battery box, updated its structural models in SolidWorks, and spot welded battery modules. Outside the team I design and build my own projects, from a 3D-printed bracket to an expandable home lighting system.

I'm looking for mechanical engineering internships starting summer 2027 and co-op opportunities starting fall 2027, where I can design, build, and test real hardware.
