Overview

The problem

I wanted to control every light in the house from one place, by voice or phone, without paying for a closed commercial hub.

I designed and installed a whole-home lighting automation system run by a Raspberry Pi 5 on Homebridge, which lets the lights work with Apple HomePod and iOS devices. I soldered and mounted all the electronic modules and controllers myself.
