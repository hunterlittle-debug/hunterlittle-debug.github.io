Overview

The project

A family member uses a CPAP machine, and the tubing kept getting in the way. I wanted to make something that would hold the tube up and out of the way.

I designed a bracket in FreeCAD and printed it in PLA on my Creality printer. Once it was installed, the tubing stopped getting in the way and the machine was easier to use.

One end hooks over the headboard and the other end has a channel that the hose sits in. No tools or screws needed.

What it had to do

  • Fit the hose snugly
  • Keep the tubing out of the way
  • Hang on the headboard without tools or screws
  • Not damage the tubing, which is pretty delicate
  • Print on my Creality printer in PLA

Decisions

Design choices

The main calls I made and why.

01

Why FreeCAD

I modeled the whole thing in FreeCAD.

Why: Honestly, it was the only free option and I was on a budget.

02

Printing it

I printed it in PLA on my Creality Ender-3 Pro. Each print took about 2 hours.

Why: I already had the printer and PLA, and at 2 hours a print I could try a new version the same day.

03

Six versions

It took six tries to get it right.

Why: Each version fixed one thing. First I had to get it to hold onto the headboard. Then I matched the channel to the size of the hose. Then I made sure it held the hose without damaging it. The last one was a thinner version for travel.

Reflection

What I learned

It took six versions to get this right. Printing each one and testing it on the real headboard showed me what to fix next. The travel version only happened because I thought about how it would actually get used.
