Overview

The problem

CPAP tubing tends to get in the way: it drapes, tangles, and pulls on the mask. I wanted a simple fixture that would hold the tube in a better position and route it out of the way.

I designed a structural bracket in FreeCAD, 3D printed it in PLA on a Creality printer, and installed it. It eliminated the tubing interference and made the setup easier to use day to day.
